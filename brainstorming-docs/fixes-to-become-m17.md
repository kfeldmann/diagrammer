## M16 Findings (all minor; nothing blocking)

1. **`assign_lanes` group-merge isn't the documented "union by interval merge"** (`src/layout.rs:~3570`): membership is tested only against the group's *head* span, not the running union. Chained overlaps (A[0,10], B[5,15], C[12,20]) leave C unmerged despite overlapping B. Worse, a group's members can pairwise overlap the head but not each other (A[0,10], C[0.5,1], B[9,9.5]), making the intersection empty — then `gap_lo > gap_hi` and every member gets lane `gap_lo`, which can sit *outside* a member's own gap (edge C's jog at 9 while its gap is [0.5,1]). Deterministic and likely self-corrected by the router/repair loop, but worth a guard (track running `(min lo, max hi)` during the sweep; fall back to per-edge own-gap midpoints when the intersection is empty). Would change output → snapshots would need regeneration, so I left it alone pending your call.

2. **"Verified" claim has no durable test:** milestones.md states phase-1 polylines are identical under edge permutations, but no automated test enshrines it (I only confirmed manually). A small permutation test would make the claim regression-proof. Also worth being aware: *final* (post-phase-2) routes **do** still differ under permutation on `sides`/`cluster` — that's the documented bounded residue (phase 2 is index-ordered by design), but it slightly tempers the "quality stops depending on declaration order" headline.

3. **Debug scaffolding left in production code:** `DIAG_DBG` eprintln blocks in `assemble` (`:1123`) and `scan_violations` (`:3005,3039`), plus `DIAG_SRC` in the ignored dump test. Harmless (no determinism impact, env lookup cost negligible), but undocumented anywhere — either document in AGENTS.md or strip before release. Note the dump prints pre-margin canonical coordinates, which is confusing if you compare against SVG output.
